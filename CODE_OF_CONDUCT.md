# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Human Exception a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus disagreement on ideas and outcomes, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and repair harm where possible.
- Respect privacy and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, personal attacks, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project owner through the contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
